Why now

The demand is here. The rules aren't.

AI created enormous demand for human voice — accents, languages, emotional range, conversational nuance. But most voice data is still acquired through fragmented deals that leave talent confused and companies exposed.

We think the answer isn't slowing AI down. It's building the licensing layer it was missing.

The Getty Images parallel

Before Getty, photographers couldn't license at scale. Getty built the marketplace, the contracts, the trust — and everyone won.

Moody Pines is building that framework for voice.
