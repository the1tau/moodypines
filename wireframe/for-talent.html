Your questions, answered

Five things every voice professional asks us.

Source: these five map directly to the talent concerns in the brand doc — pay, amount, control, usage, trust. Each gets a plain, honest answer.
01

Will I get paid?

Yes. Every license is a paid license, with clear, fair terms agreed up front.

02

How much?

Transparent rates by usage and scope — no mystery deductions, no hidden downstream deals.

03

Who controls my voice?

You do. You set what your voice can and can't be used for — and you can revoke consent.

04

What can it be used for?

Only what you approve. Usage is scoped in the contract and tracked end to end.

05

Can I trust this company?

Provenance and attribution travel with your voice. You can see where it goes, always.
