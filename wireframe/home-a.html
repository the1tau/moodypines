The problem

AI created enormous demand for voice. The system to license it fairly was never built.

Voice datasets are acquired through fragmented processes that leave everyone exposed — talent who lose control, and companies who inherit risk.

Voice talent fear

"Where is my voice going?"

Ownership · Compensation · Future usage · Attribution · Long-term control. Today, none of it is clear.

AI companies fear

"Can we actually use this?"

Consent · Provenance · Rights management · Legal exposure · Litigation risk. The data is only as good as its paperwork.
