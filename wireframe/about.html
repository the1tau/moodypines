Our mission

Helping human voices thrive in the age of AI.

AI learned to speak from people. We're building the rights, licensing, and data infrastructure that makes sure those people are paid, credited, and in control — and that the companies building with voice can do it responsibly.

Positioning anchor: this is the emotional positioning line verbatim from the brand doc. The whole page resolves the tension — advocate and infrastructure.
DOCUMENTARY · wide studio scene, talent + engineer mid-session

Why we exist

The demand came fast. The fairness didn't.

AI created enormous demand for human voice — every accent, language, and shade of emotion. But the way that voice gets acquired is still fragmented and opaque, leaving talent confused and companies exposed.

We don't think the answer is to slow AI down. We think it's to build the licensing layer it was missing — the way Getty Images once did for photography.
