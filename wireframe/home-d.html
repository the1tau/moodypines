For AI companies

Why Moody Pines is superior to existing voice sources.

For AI companies →

AI teams have been forced to source voice data by scraping, cloning, or stitching together one-off deals. Moody Pines provides premium voice data sourced directly from professional talent, with the rights, quality, and provenance required to build products with confidence.

Sourcing voice for AI
The Moody Pines way
The traditional way
Quality
Studio-grade recordings from vetted professionals — the bar others train toward.
Variable — scraped, generic, or synthetic audio of uneven fidelity.
Breadth
Deep range across languages, dialects, emotional registers, and linguistic effects — the variety a convincing voice model actually needs.
A narrow set of voices and languages — thin on accent, emotion, and delivery. Models trained on it can land flat or uncanny.
Legal exposure
Licensed directly from the artist, with documented consent to their biometric likeness. Defensible under due diligence.
Scraped audio or third-party vendors — no clear chain to the person. If the rights don't hold, the liability is yours.
Time to usable data
A finished, rights-cleared dataset, off the shelf.
6–12 months building relationships, negotiating one-offs, and recording from scratch.
The relationship
We hold the artist relationship and the legal framework, so you don't have to build it.
Teams avoid direct artist relationships by design, leaving the rights ambiguous.
